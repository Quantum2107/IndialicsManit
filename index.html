ABOUT THE CONFERENCE

About the Conference

Connecting researchers, policymakers, innovators and industry
to shape the future of innovation in the Global South.

The IndiaLICS Annual International Conference 2026 brings together researchers, policymakers, industry leaders and innovators to explore emerging perspectives on innovation, sustainable development and inclusive growth in the Global South. The conference fosters interdisciplinary dialogue on regional innovation systems, infrastructure, technology, public policy and emerging development challenges.

Hosted at Maulana Azad National Institute of Technology (MANIT), Bhopal, the conference provides a vibrant platform for presenting cutting-edge research, sharing best practices and building meaningful partnerships through keynote sessions, technical presentations and collaborative discussions.
